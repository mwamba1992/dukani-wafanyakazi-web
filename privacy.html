1. Sisi ni nani

Programu inaendeshwa na [COMPANY NAME, ADDRESS] ("Dukani", "sisi"). Barua pepe: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Nani anawajibika kwa taarifa zipi

  • Mwajiri (biashara) ndiye mdhibiti wa taarifa za wafanyakazi wake: yeye ndiye anayeamua nani aongezwe, mshahara, zamu, na anayeidhinisha maombi. Sisi tunachakata taarifa hizo kwa niaba yake, kwa maelekezo yake (mchakataji).
  • Dukani ndiye mdhibiti wa taarifa za akaunti zinazohitajika kuendesha huduma yenyewe — kwa mfano usajili wa mmiliki, kuingia, usalama na tokeni za arifa.

Kwa lugha rahisi: kama wewe ni mfanyakazi, maswali kuhusu mshahara, mahudhurio au rekodi zako yaende kwanza kwa mwajiri wako. Sisi tutamsaidia, na unaweza pia kutuandikia moja kwa moja.

2. Taarifa tunazokusanya

Akaunti za wafanyakazi zinafunguliwa na mwajiri (anakupa namba ya simu na PIN). Mmiliki anajisajili mwenyewe.

UtambulishoJina, namba ya simu, cheo/kazi.

3. Tunazitumia kwa nini

  • Kukuwezesha kuingia na kulinda akaunti yako.
  • Kurekodi mahudhurio na kuonyesha kama ulikuwa dukani (GPS) au la.
  • Kushughulikia maombi ya advance na likizo.
  • Kuhesabu mishahara na makato, kutengeneza slipu (PDF) na ripoti za mwezi.
  • Kutuma arifa (k.m. "ombi limekubaliwa", "slipu iko tayari").
  • Kuendesha, kurekebisha hitilafu na kulinda huduma; kutimiza wajibu wa kisheria.

Hatutumii taarifa zako kwa matangazo wala kufanya maamuzi ya kiotomatiki yenye athari kubwa kwako; maamuzi (k.m. kukubali advance) hufanywa na mwajiri.

4. Msingi wa kisheria

Chini ya Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi, 2022 (PDPA):

  • Mkataba — kutoa huduma kwa biashara na kwa wewe kama mtumiaji.
  • Wajibu wa kisheria — mwajiri lazima atunze rekodi za ajira, mishahara na kodi.
  • Maslahi halali — usalama, kuzuia udanganyifu wa mahudhurio, kuboresha huduma.
  • Ridhaa — kwa GPS, kamera (selfie) na arifa, kupitia ruhusa za simu yako. Unaweza kuiondoa wakati wowote kwenye mipangilio ya simu.

5. Tunashirikisha nani

  • Mwajiri wako — mmiliki na mameneja wa biashara yako wanaona mahudhurio (pamoja na hali ya GPS na umbali), selfie, maombi na mishahara yako. Wafanyakazi wengine hawaoni taarifa zako.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — kutuma arifa kwenye simu yako; hupokea tokeni ya kifaa na maandishi ya arifa.
  • Mtoa huduma ya seva (hosting) — anayehifadhi seva yetu na hifadhi rudufu (backup).
  • Mamlaka — pale tu sheria inapotutaka (k.m. amri ya mahakama).

Hatuuzi taarifa binafsi kwa mtu yeyote.

6. Taarifa nje ya Tanzania

Seva yetu iko [SERVER LOCATION]. Google/Firebase inaweza kuchakata tokeni za arifa nje ya Tanzania. Tunapohamisha taarifa nje ya nchi tunachukua hatua zinazotakiwa na PDPA ili ziwe na ulinzi unaofaa.

7. Muda wa kuhifadhi

  • Tunahifadhi taarifa wakati akaunti ya biashara iko hai.
  • Rekodi za mahudhurio na mishahara zinahifadhiwa kwa muda ambao mwajiri anazihitaji kisheria — hadi miaka 5 baada ya ajira kuisha (Sheria ya Ajira na Mahusiano Kazini) na miaka 5 kwa rekodi za kodi (Sheria ya Usimamizi wa Kodi).
  • Biashara ikifunga akaunti, tunafuta au kuficha utambulisho wa taarifa ndani ya siku 90, isipokuwa sheria ikitutaka kuzihifadhi zaidi.
  • Selfie zinafutwa baada ya siku 90.
  • Hifadhi rudufu (backup) huzunguka na kufutwa baada ya siku 14.

8. Usalama

  • Mawasiliano yote yamesimbwa kwa HTTPS.
  • PIN zinahifadhiwa kama hash ya bcrypt; majaribio mengi ya PIN yasiyo sahihi yanazuiwa kwa muda.
  • Ruhusa kulingana na nafasi (mmiliki, meneja, mfanyakazi); kila biashara inaona taarifa zake tu.
  • Usiwape wengine PIN yako. Ukihisi imevuja, ibadilishe ("Badili PIN") au mwambie mwajiri wako.

9. Haki zako

Chini ya PDPA 2022 una haki ya:

  • kupata nakala ya taarifa zako;
  • kurekebisha taarifa zisizo sahihi;
  • kuomba kufutwa kwa taarifa (isipokuwa zile ambazo mwajiri lazima azihifadhi kisheria);
  • kupinga au kuzuia matumizi fulani;
  • kuondoa ridhaa (GPS, kamera, arifa) kwenye mipangilio ya simu.

Tuandikie [CONTACT EMAIL] au mwombe mwajiri wako. Tutajibu ndani ya siku 30 na tunaweza kuthibitisha utambulisho wako kwanza. Ukiwa hujaridhika, unaweza kulalamika kwa Tume ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi (PDPC).

1. Who we are

The app is operated by [COMPANY NAME, ADDRESS] ("Dukani", "we"). Email: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Who is responsible for what

  • The employer (the business) is the controller of its employees' data: it decides who is added, salaries, shifts, and approves requests. We process that data on its behalf and on its instructions (as processor).
  • Dukani is the controller of the account data needed to run the service itself — for example owner sign-up, sign-in, security and notification tokens.

In plain words: if you are an employee, questions about your pay, attendance or records should go to your employer first. We will help them, and you can also write to us directly.

2. Data we collect

Employee accounts are created by the employer (who gives you a phone number and PIN). Owners sign up themselves.

IdentityName, phone number, job title.
PIN4-digit PIN — stored only as a bcrypt hash; we cannot read it.
PayrollGross salary, NSSF number (optional), payroll figures (NSSF, PAYE, SDL, WCF), payslips, and a record that the salary was paid (method and reference).
RequestsSalary advance and leave requests: amount, reason, dates and decision.
AttendanceClock-in and clock-out times, lateness, hours worked, method (your phone or the shared device).
Location (GPS)Only at the moment you clock in or out: latitude, longitude, accuracy and distance to the branch. We do not track you in the background or at any other time. GPS is optional — if you decline, you are still clocked in, marked "No GPS".
SelfieOptional photo at clock-in ("Clock in with a selfie"). Stored on our server and visible only to your business's owner and managers.
DeviceA random device ID generated by the app, and a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
BusinessBusiness name and phone, branches and their locations, settings (e.g. shifts, advance limit).

No ads, no analytics SDKs, no advertising tracking. We do not sell your data.

3. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your account secure.
  • To record attendance and show whether you were at the shop (GPS) or not.
  • To handle salary advance and leave requests.
  • To calculate pay and deductions, and produce payslips (PDF) and monthly reports.
  • To send notifications (e.g. "request approved", "payslip ready").
  • To run, fix and protect the service, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions with significant effects on you; decisions (e.g. approving an advance) are made by your employer.

4. Legal bases

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (PDPA):

  • Contract — to provide the service to the business and to you as a user.
  • Legal obligation — employers must keep employment, payroll and tax records.
  • Legitimate interests — security, preventing attendance fraud, improving the service.
  • Consent — for GPS, camera (selfie) and notifications, through your phone's permissions. You can withdraw it at any time in your phone settings.

5. Who we share it with

  • Your employer — your business's owner and managers see your attendance (including GPS status and distance), selfies, requests and payroll. Other workers do not see your data.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — to deliver notifications to your phone; it receives the device token and the notification text.
  • Our hosting provider — which hosts our server and backups.
  • Authorities — only when the law requires it (e.g. a court order).

We do not sell personal data to anyone.

6. Transfers outside Tanzania

Our server is located in [SERVER LOCATION]. Google/Firebase may process push tokens outside Tanzania. When data is transferred abroad we take the steps the PDPA requires so it stays adequately protected.

7. How long we keep it

  • We keep data while the business account is active.
  • Attendance and payroll records are kept as long as the employer needs them by law — up to 5 years after employment ends (Employment and Labour Relations Act) and 5 years for tax records (Tax Administration Act).
  • When a business closes its account, we delete or anonymise its data within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Selfies are deleted after 90 days.
  • Backups rotate and are deleted after 14 days.

8. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • PINs are stored as bcrypt hashes; repeated wrong PIN attempts are temporarily locked out.
  • Role-based access (owner, manager, worker); each business sees only its own data.
  • Do not share your PIN. If you think it has leaked, change it ("Change PIN") or tell your employer.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA 2022 you have the right to:

  • access a copy of your data;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • ask for deletion (except data your employer must keep by law);
  • object to or restrict certain uses;
  • withdraw consent (GPS, camera, notifications) in your phone settings.

Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] or ask your employer. We reply within 30 days and may first verify your identity. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) of Tanzania.

10. Deleting your account and data

Employees: ask your employer to remove you from the team, or email [CONTACT EMAIL] from a known phone number or email address with your name, phone number and business name.

Owners: email [CONTACT EMAIL] asking to close your business account; we will verify it is you.

Once verified, we delete the account, PIN, device tokens and selfies straight away. Attendance and payroll records that the employer must keep by law stay until that period ends (see section 7), then they are deleted.

11. Children

This is a workplace app and is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data; if we find any, we delete it.
